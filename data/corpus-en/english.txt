Good morning. The weather report for tomorrow calls for fog over the airfield until noon, with visibility under five hundred yards. All reconnaissance flights will stay on the ground until the fog lifts. The wind will come from the west at ten to fifteen miles an hour, and rain is expected in the evening.

Hello, how are you? I hope you are well and that the trip home was not too long. We are all fine here. The children started school last week and they already have new friends. Your mother sends her love and asks when you will come to visit again. Write when you can and tell us about the new house.

Meet me at the train station at eight o'clock tomorrow night. Bring the package and do not tell anyone where you are going. If I am late, wait for me by the clock near the main entrance. I will be wearing a gray coat and a dark hat.

The convoy left the harbor at dawn and is now heading north at eight knots. Two destroyers and four smaller ships are escorting it. Enemy aircraft were seen near the coast in the afternoon, but no attack was made. We expect to reach port the day after tomorrow if the weather holds.

Our company reached the river this morning and found the bridge still standing. The engineers are checking it now. The enemy withdrew during the night and left behind trucks, guns and supplies. We took twenty prisoners, including one officer. Our losses are light. We need more fuel and ammunition before we can move on.

Dear Sarah, thank you for your letter. It arrived on Tuesday, just as the snow began. The town looks beautiful under a white blanket, but the roads are closed and the mail will be slow for a while. I have been reading the books you sent me, and I think of you every evening when I light the lamp by the window.

The meeting has been moved to Thursday at ten in the morning. Please bring the reports from last month and the new plans for the factory. The director wants to discuss the budget for next year and the delays in the northern division. Coffee will be served in the conference room.

Nothing to report. The night was quiet along the whole front. Patrols went out after midnight and returned before dawn without contact. The telephone line to headquarters was cut by artillery fire and has been repaired. Morale is good, and the men are resting.

We landed in the city late in the evening and took a taxi to the hotel. The streets were full of people walking, eating and talking in the warm air. After dinner we walked down to the river and watched the lights on the water. Tomorrow we will visit the old museum and the market near the cathedral.

The secret of good bread is patience. Mix the flour, water, salt and yeast, then let the dough rest for several hours. Fold it gently, shape it into a loaf and bake it in a very hot oven. When the crust is brown and the bottom sounds hollow, the bread is ready.

Attention all units. The attack will begin at five thirty tomorrow morning after a short artillery barrage. The first battalion will advance on the right and the second on the left. Tanks will follow the first battalion and wait for orders. Keep radio silence until the attack begins.

I am sorry I could not call you yesterday. The phone lines were down all day because of the storm. Everything is fine now, and the power came back this morning. Let us talk on Sunday after lunch, if that works for you. I have some good news that I want to tell you myself.

Science has changed the way we live in the last hundred years. Electricity, the telephone and the radio made it possible to send messages across the world in seconds. With that speed came a new need: to keep important messages secret from people who were not meant to read them.

The ship will arrive at the port on Friday evening. Please have the trucks ready at the north pier by seven o'clock. The cargo includes machine parts, medicine and food for the hospital. Tell the captain that the customs papers are in the blue folder in my office.

Happy birthday, my friend. I hope this year brings you health, luck and many good days with the people you love. We will celebrate together when you are back in town. Until then, raise a glass for me, and do not work too hard.

Weather forecast for the coast: winds from the northwest, strong in the afternoon, with heavy seas and rain showers. Visibility will be poor at times. Small boats should stay in harbor until Sunday. Temperatures will fall sharply overnight.

The library is open every day except Monday from nine in the morning until six in the evening. Students may borrow up to five books for two weeks. Rare books must be read in the reading room and cannot be taken home. Please keep your voice low and turn off your phone.

We have lost contact with the third company since last night. Their last known position was near the old mill by the stream. Send a runner at once and report whether the company is surrounded and whether it needs help. Enemy tanks were heard moving in the woods to the east.

I walked along the beach this morning and watched the fishermen pull their boats onto the sand. The sea was calm and the sky was bright. A group of children were building a castle near the water, and a dog ran in circles around them, barking at the waves.

The new password for this week is eagle, and the answer is sun. Pass it on by word of mouth only. Guards who are approached without the password must report at once to the officer on duty.
